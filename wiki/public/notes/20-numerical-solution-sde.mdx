_"The aim of this course is to equip students with the skills to conduct simulations and perform mathematical convergence analysis for stochastic models, with applications in fields such as mathematical finance. 
<br/>The course provides a comprehensive understanding of various numerical methods, covering their foundational principles, convergence properties, and practical implementation considerations." (from the official course's page)_

The course has been taught at <a href="https://ethz.ch/en.html" target="_blank">ETH Zurich</a> by <a href="https://people.math.ethz.ch/~jteichma/" target="_blank">Professor Josef TeichmannJosef Teichmann</a> and <a href="https://www.lse.ac.uk/people/christoph-czichowsky" target="_blank">Dr Christoph Czichowsky</a>
